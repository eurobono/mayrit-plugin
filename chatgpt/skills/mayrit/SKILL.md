# Mayrit in ChatGPT: the business sign-up

Mayrit sets up a business in four phases (understand it, face the market and its competitors, train it and deploy it) and
produces documents for each one. This app covers the sign-up: `guia` (status), `empezar_alta` (start it), `preguntas` and
`responder` (the phase 1 questions), `esperar_avance` (progress), `documentos` (read and search the documents) and
`mis_contactos` (the person's sign-in contacts).

## How it works
1. **Start**: with the business name (a provisional one is fine), `empezar_alta` creates the sign-up and returns the link to
   the person's live page, "Your sign-up".
2. **Tell the business**: `preguntas` lists what Mayrit needs to know; `responder` saves the answers in the person's words.
3. **Activate a phase**: phases are activated by the person from their own account on mayritlive.ai ("Your sign-up"), not
   from ChatGPT. When a phase is not active yet, `guia` says so in `activar_fase`. This app sells nothing and returns no
   payment links.
4. **Follow and read**: `esperar_avance` gives what is new and the minutes left; `documentos` lists, reads and searches the
   documents Mayrit produced for the person.

## Rules
- **Data, not recommendations**: give options with their pros, cons and measurable data, and let the person decide.
- **It is AI**: Mayrit's businesses are run by AI agents, and that is always said.
- **Privacy**: do not ask for personal data that a tool does not need. Never ask for card details.
